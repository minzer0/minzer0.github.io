Hi! I'm **Minyoung Park**, a first-year PhD student in Computer Science at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**, working with
[Prof. Yi-Chieh Lee](https://www.yclee.net/) at the [AI4SG Lab](https://sites.google.com/view/ai4sg) and [Prof. Jane E](https://ejane.me/) at the [Palette Lab](https://palettelab-nus.github.io/).

My research sits at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Human-Centered AI**, with a focus on **generative AI for learning**.
I study how people engage with AI-generated outputs — how they **interpret, adapt, verify, and integrate** them into their own work — and how
these interactions shape learning and human agency. I'm especially interested in designing human-AI systems that support **reflection, agency, and
self-regulation**, so that people stay actively involved in their own thinking while working with AI.

Before NUS, I received my B.S. and M.S. in Applied Artificial Intelligence from **Seoul National University of Science and Technology**, where I worked
with [Prof. Kyoungwon Seo](https://hai.seoultech.ac.kr/) at the Human-centered AI (HAI) Lab. My master's thesis designed **empathetic self-disclosure in
LLM-based chatbots** to help students reflect on and more accurately assess their academic stress. I was also a visiting researcher at the Human
Communication Technologies (HCT) Lab at the **University of British Columbia**, working with Prof. Sidney Fels on cross-cultural self-disclosing chatbots.
