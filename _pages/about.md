Hi! I'm **Minyoung Park**, a PhD student in Computer Science at the **National University of Singapore (NUS)**.

My research lies at the intersection of **Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)** and **Human-Centered AI**, with a particular interest in **generative AI for learning**. I study how people engage with AI-generated outputs—how they **interpret, adapt, verify, and integrate** them into their own work—and how these interactions shape learning and human agency.

My current research explores how human-AI systems can support **reflection, agency, and self-regulation**, helping people engage with generative AI while remaining actively involved in their own thinking and learning.

Previously, I studied **conversational AI for supporting students' self-reflection** at the Human-centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) Lab at Seoul National University of Science and Technology. I was also a visiting researcher at the Human Communication Technologies (HCT) Lab at the University of British Columbia.
