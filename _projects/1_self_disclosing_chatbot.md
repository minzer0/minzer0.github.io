**2024 – 2025 · Lead Researcher · [HAI Lab](https://hai.seoultech.ac.kr/index.html), SeoulTech**

🤝 **With:** [Institute for Information & Communications Technology Planning & Evaluation (IITP)](https://www.iitp.kr/) · international collaboration with the [HCT Lab](https://hct-lab.github.io), UBC

Ask students about their worries and you often get a word or two. This project asked: what if the chatbot opened up first?

We designed an LLM-based chatbot that uses **empathetic self-disclosure**, sharing relevant experiences and thoughts before asking questions, and
embedded the SISCO Inventory of Academic Stress into the conversation. In a randomized experiment with **50 university students**, compared with an
otherwise identical chatbot without self-disclosure, the self-disclosing chatbot:

- elicited **longer, richer conversations** (≈15.6 vs. 11.3 minutes; ≈240 vs. 162 words),
- produced **more accurate stress assessments** (0.94 vs. 0.86 agreement within ±1 point of the standard inventory), and
- led students to name stressors more concretely and make **more actionable coping plans**.

This work became my master's thesis, a patent application, and papers at IJETHE, CHI 2025 (LBW), and HCI Korea 2025 (Best Paper Award).
