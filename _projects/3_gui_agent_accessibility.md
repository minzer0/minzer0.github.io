**Sep 2025 – Feb 2026 · Research Assistant · [HAI Lab](https://hai.seoultech.ac.kr/index.html), SeoulTech**

🤝 **With:** [National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)](https://www.nrf.re.kr/)

Accessibility evaluation is slow, expert-dependent work. This project explores whether AI agents that operate real interfaces can help automate it.

- Designed a GUI agent capable of performing **expert-level automated evaluations of UX accessibility**
- Planned and implemented experimental protocols for validating **human–AI comparative performance** in accessibility assessments
