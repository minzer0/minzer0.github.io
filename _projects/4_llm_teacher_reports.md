**2023 – 2025 · Research Assistant · [HAI Lab](https://hai.seoultech.ac.kr/index.html), SeoulTech**

🧪 **HAI Lab research project** (2023 – 2024), carried out alongside the 💰 [IITP](https://www.iitp.kr/)-funded program *AI Copilot Technologies for Adaptive, Teacher-Augmented Learning in Future Classrooms* (2023 – 2025)

As part of a larger effort on AI copilots for adaptive, teacher-augmented classrooms:

- Fine-tuned an LLM to generate **student competency analysis reports** and validated its effectiveness through expert interviews
- Identified the potential and limitations of LLMs in supporting competency assessment and report generation
- Built a counseling chatbot and analysis models supporting AI-driven student coaching, and co-developed an integrated platform for personalized,
  teacher-augmented learning

📄 [HCI Korea 2024 paper](https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE11714640)
