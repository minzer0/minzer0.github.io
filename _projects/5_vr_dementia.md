**2022 – 2024 · Research Assistant · [HAI Lab](https://hai.seoultech.ac.kr/index.html), SeoulTech**

💰 **Funded by:** [National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)](https://www.nrf.re.kr/) · 🏥 **Clinical partner:** Hanyang University Guri Hospital

- Collected daily VR kiosk interaction data from participants with mild cognitive impairment (MCI) and healthy controls, in collaboration with
  Hanyang University Guri Hospital
- Built multimodal, VR behavior–based predictive models to distinguish MCI from healthy controls
