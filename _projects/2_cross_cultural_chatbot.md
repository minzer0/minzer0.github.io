**Jan – Feb 2025 · Visiting Researcher (Lead Researcher) · [HCT Lab](https://hct-lab.github.io), University of British Columbia**

🤝 **With:** [HCT Lab](https://hct-lab.github.io), University of British Columbia · [Institute for Information & Communications Technology Planning & Evaluation (IITP)](https://www.iitp.kr/)

Working with [Prof. Sidney Fels](https://ece.ubc.ca/sid-fels/), I extended the self-disclosing chatbot to a new cultural context to investigate how interactions with self-disclosing
chatbots vary across cultures.

- Conducted pilot studies with UBC students
- Analyzed conversation logs and interview data to compare engagement and reflection patterns across cultural groups
